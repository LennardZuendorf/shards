Shardsflint design system

Flint

A calm, mono identity for Shards, the task manager humans and agents share over Markdown you own. The bot art carries colour, so nothing else has to. Ember means one thing: a person is needed.

Mono by defaultSurfaces are slate. Stones tint them; they never add a second accent.
One signalEmber = needs you (overdue, needs-input). Fixed in every stone.
Three identitiesProjects are shapes, people are circles, agents wear their art.
Two modesQuiet by default; Focus (⌘\) hides everything but the list.

Chosen not to do

No glass frameSolid mono reads calmer, and it removes the vibrancy fallback problem on Linux.
No per-project colourShapes work without colour vision and keep the bot art as the only colour.
No pixel icons or pixel fontToo noisy in daily use; pixels live only in agent art and the loader.
No second accentEmber has exactly one meaning. "Working" uses the stone signal.
No light blue or purpleBlue belongs to sibling projects; purple is the generic AI look.
No “Obsidian” nameIt would read as a nod to the Obsidian app in a Markdown tool.
